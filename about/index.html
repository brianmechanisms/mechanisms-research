About Mechanisms Showcase

Our Research

We conduct research and development in mechanical mechanisms, currently focusing on self-powered long-range linear motion mechanisms for applications requiring extended displacement capabilities with varying precision requirements.

Our research methodology combines theoretical analysis, kinematic synthesis, and prototype development to advance the field of mechanical motion systems.

The mechanisms presented here represent concepts and findings from our research into self-powered long-range linear motion systems for applications requiring extended displacement capabilities with varying precision requirements.

What We Offer

  • ▸
    Comprehensive Collection: Explore dozens of mechanical mechanisms ranging from simple linkages to complex gear systems.
  • ▸
    Visual Learning: Each mechanism includes static diagrams and animated demonstrations to clearly show how it operates.
  • ▸
    Technical Details: In-depth descriptions, parameters, advantages, disadvantages, and real-world applications.
  • ▸
    Code Examples: Python code samples for simulating and analyzing mechanical movements programmatically.

Categories

Our mechanisms are organized into several main categories:

Reciprocating Mechanisms

Convert rotary motion to linear motion and vice versa

Gear Systems

Power transmission and speed modification mechanisms

Cam Mechanisms

Precise motion control through cam profiles

Linkages

Four-bar and multi-bar linkage systems
