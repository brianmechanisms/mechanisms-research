Design Specifications

Loci · parametric paths

Closed-form planar curves A(t), t ∈ [0, 1], specified independently of any mechanism that realises them. A locus is the design specification — position, velocity, and derived constraints — that downstream cam, gear, or linkage syntheses consume.
