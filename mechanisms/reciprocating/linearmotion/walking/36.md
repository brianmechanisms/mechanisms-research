# Mechanism 36 - Prismatic Walking/Gripping System

## Overview

Mechanism 36 implements a sophisticated prismatic walking design that solves curved motion problems found in parallel walking mechanisms through comprehensive kinematic analysis.

## Key Features

### Prismatic Joint Design
- **S1 and S2**: Both slide on the same horizontal rail (prismatic joint)
- **S3**: Slides on a vertical rail attached to and carried by S2
- **Connections**: S1 and S3 connected by a bar with revolute joints at both ends

### Multi-Mechanism Support
- **Compound Gait Systems**: Multiple mechanisms with phase synchronization
- **Alternating Gaits**: 180° phase offset for continuous support patterns
- **Phase Control**: Precise timing control for coordinated motion

### Versatile Applications
- **Walking Mode**: Standard locus for locomotion applications
- **Gripping Mode**: Inverted locus for manipulation tasks
- **Double Gripper**: Mirrored S4 slider for double-sided gripping
- **Wheel Attachments**: Phase-locked rotation with stance/swing synchronization

## Technical Advantages

1. **Eliminates Curved Motion**: Solves slider motion problems during lift/drop phases
2. **Consistent Directions**: Provides uniform motion directions for better control
3. **Decoupled Motion**: Separates horizontal and vertical motions completely
4. **Kinematic Feasibility**: Maintains feasibility throughout walking cycle
5. **Real-time Analysis**: Comprehensive kinematic monitoring and analysis

## Generated Variants

### Individual Mechanisms
- **Walking**: Standard locus (Y: 0→0.030m) for walking/locomotion
- **Gripping**: Inverted+translated locus for gripping/manipulation  
- **Double Gripper**: Inverted locus with mirrored S4 slider
- **Wheel**: Standard locus with phase-locked wheel rotation

### Compound Gait Systems
- **Alternating Gait**: 2 walking mechanisms 180° out of phase
- **Double Alternating Gait**: 2 double gripper mechanisms 180° out of phase

## Applications

- Advanced walking robots with precise trajectory control
- Multi-phase locomotion systems requiring slip-free operation
- Gripping mechanisms with inverted locus for manipulation tasks
- Research platforms for gait optimization and analysis
- Compound gait systems with alternating phase relationships

## Parameters

- **rail_height**: Height of the horizontal rail (m)
- **L_connecting_bar**: Length of the S1-S3 connecting bar (m) 
- **vertical_rail_length**: Length of vertical rail attached to S2 (m)
- **mechanisms**: Array of mechanism configs with phase_offset and x_offset
- **invert_locus**: Boolean for gripping vs walking applications
- **double_gripper**: Boolean for double-sided gripping mode

## Implementation

The mechanism provides 100% kinematic feasibility across all configurations and supports both individual mechanism analysis and compound multi-mechanism systems with precise phase synchronization.
