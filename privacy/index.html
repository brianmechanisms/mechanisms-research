Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

This site, cseco.co.ke/mechanisms-research, is run by Brian Mechanisms Robotics (Brian Onang'o). Questions about this policy or your data: surgbc@gmail.com.

Public pages

The public pages (home, about, research overview, public) collect no personal data. They use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Signing in to the research sections

The research sections are open only to people we have invited. Sign-in is handled by Cloudflare Access, either with "Sign in with Google" or with a one-time code sent to your email address.

  • What we receive: your email address (and, with Google, your name and Google account ID, through the basic openid, email and profile scopes). We request no other Google data and no access to your Google account.
  • What we use it for: only to check that your email address is on our list of invited people and to keep you signed in. It is not used for marketing, not sold and not shared, except with the services that run the sign-in (below).
  • Cookies: Cloudflare Access sets a sign-in cookie (CF_Authorization) that keeps you signed in for up to 24 hours. "Sign out" removes it.
  • Logs: Cloudflare keeps a log of sign-ins (email address, time, IP address and country) for security, under its own retention rules.

Services involved

  • Cloudflare (sign-in, hosting of the research sections): cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Google (only if you choose Sign in with Google): policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub Pages (hosting of the public pages): docs.github.com/site-policy

Your choices

You can ask us to remove your email address from the invited list at any time by writing to surgbc@gmail.com; you will then no longer be able to sign in. You can also revoke this site's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
