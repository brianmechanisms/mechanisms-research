Research Overview

An overview of our research program in self-powered long-range linear motion mechanisms.

Primary Research Objective

Our research focuses on the design and development of self-powered long-range linear motion mechanisms. These mechanisms are engineered for applications requiring extended linear displacement capabilities withvarying precision requirements rather than ultra-high accuracy applications.

Technical Framework

Foot Locus Design Methodology:

  • • Horizontal Velocity Constraints: Vx(terminal_drop) = Vx(stance) = Vx(initial_rise) = K
  • • Foot Vertical Stability: Vy(foot, stance) = 0
  • • Body Static Balancing: Vy(body) = 0 throughout entire cycle
  • • Acceleration Continuity: lim(t→transition) da/dt → 0 (jerk minimization)
  • • Fixed Stride Length: Optimized for guided rail applications
